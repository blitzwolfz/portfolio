turn the page

Portrait.
Landscape.

A page that fits your hands. Play upright or turn the page sideways, with native layouts for both and the same one-press moves.

Tap on iPhone or Android. Click or press Space on Mac; Android also supports keyboard and D-pad input.

frame rate

Built to keep up.

Quick jumps. Long drops. A notebook that keeps moving.

Existing iPhone and Mac builds support up to 120 FPS on supported hardware and displays. Android requests the display’s highest supported refresh rate; physical-device measurements are pending.
