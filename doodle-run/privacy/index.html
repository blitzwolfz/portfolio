Privacy

This page describes the native doodle-run app for iOS, macOS and Android.

Information on your device

The app stores campaign progress, best distance and your sound preference locally on your device. It does not send this gameplay information to the developer. The Android app disables cloud and device-transfer backup.

No account or tracking

The game does not include an account system, advertising, analytics services, crash-reporting services or tracking. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone. The Android app does not request network access.

When you contact support

If you email support, your email provider sends your address and the contents of your message to the support mailbox. Include only the information you want to share. For questions about information in a support message, or to request its deletion, contact saminm.qureshi@gmail.com.

This website

The native app and this website are separate. Loading a website involves requests to its hosting provider. The doodle-run pages do not include analytics scripts or a contact form. The Portfolio’s main page has its own contact form.

Contact

Privacy questions: saminm.qureshi@gmail.com.
