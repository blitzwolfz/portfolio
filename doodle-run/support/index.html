Need a hand?

For help, bugs or feedback, email saminm.qureshi@gmail.com.

Include your device, operating system, app version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please keep passwords and other sensitive information out of your message.

One press does the work.

Tap and hold on iPhone or Android. On Mac, click and hold or use Space. Android also supports Space, Enter and D-pad centre. A longer hold makes a higher jump. Small gaps and low ledges are vaulted automatically.

Press while hanging from a ledge to pull up. Press during a wall slide to kick away from the wall. Press as you stomp a walker for a boosted jump.

Stopped at a dot?

A filled-in dot is a pause block. Press again to keep running.

Try the line again.

A bubble carries you backward to a safer point. Campaign and Endless have a limited supply. Rough Book keeps the run going without a final mark.

Sound and performance

Use the sound control on the contents page to mute or unmute the game. The iPhone and Mac builds support up to 120 FPS on supported hardware and displays. Android adapts to the device display; measured Android performance results will be published only after physical-device verification.

Your progress

Campaign progress, best distance and the sound preference are saved locally on your device. There is no in-game account, cloud backup or progress-sync feature.
