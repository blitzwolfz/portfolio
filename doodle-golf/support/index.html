For help, bugs or feedback, email saminm.qureshi@gmail.com.

Include your device, operating system, app version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please keep passwords and other sensitive information out of your message.

Pull back from the ball.

On iPhone, drag anywhere on the page back from the ball and let go. On Mac, click and drag with a mouse or trackpad. The farther you pull, the more power the shot has.

Read the whole hole.

Rulers, erasers, drawing pins, ink and torn paper all change the way the ball moves. Your shot line stays on the page after each stroke, so you can use the working out to plan the next one.

Keep your round.

Campaign progress, best scores, Endless Golf progress, Daily Challenge progress and your preferences are saved locally on the device. There is no in-game account or progress-sync feature.

iPhone and Mac

The iOS app is designed for iPhone and portrait orientation. The macOS app is designed for Mac, with mouse and trackpad input; press R to start the current hole again and Esc to go back.
